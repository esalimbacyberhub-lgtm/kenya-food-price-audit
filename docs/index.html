4. How to read this

Established from the file
  • Most prices are model estimates. Only of 233 markets ever have an observed price.
  • In this release there are no observed goat or maize prices from to .
  • In that period the modelled goat median rises far above any observed goat price, then settles near 5,000 to 5,500 KES.
  • Months without observations moved more between the two releases than months with observations.
Not established
  • Whether the producer’s validation covers multi-year gaps like this one.
  • Why the food price index rises about 6.7 times between January 2023 and January 2024 while maize and beans move far less.
  • Why maize differs across releases (units or item definitions).
  • Whether close agreement between observed and modelled prices shows accuracy, or only that the model uses those observations.

This dashboard makes no forecasts and does not claim the dataset is wrong. The medians are across markets, and the markets that report are not a random sample.
